# EchoGrid Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-21

EchoGrid is a lightweight Android puzzle game. This policy describes what the app does and does not collect.

## Information Collection

EchoGrid does not require an account and does not collect personal information.

EchoGrid does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs, billing, or network services. The app does not request internet access.

## Device Features

EchoGrid uses audio playback locally on the device for game feedback. EchoGrid also uses vibration and haptics locally on the device to provide proximity feedback during gameplay.

## Feedback Emails

Players may choose to send feedback by email. Feedback emails are voluntary, are sent outside the app, and are handled through the player's email provider and the recipient mailbox.

## Data Sharing

EchoGrid does not sell personal information and does not share personal information with third parties.
