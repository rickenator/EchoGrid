# Play Store Listing Draft

## App Name

EchoGrid

## Short Description

Find the hidden target by sound. Tap the grid, hear the echo, and navigate by pitch, rhythm, and vibration.

## Full Description

EchoGrid is an audio-spatial puzzle game where every tap becomes a clue. A hidden target is placed somewhere on the grid. Tap a tile and EchoGrid answers with a two-tone echo: the first tone tells you how close you are on the X axis, and the second tone tells you how close you are on the Y axis.

Pitch, timing, heat trails, and haptic feedback combine into a learnable spatial language. Early levels are simple and forgiving. Later levels expand the grid and demand sharper listening.

Features:

- Two-tone X/Y echo feedback
- Heat trail visualization
- Haptic proximity feedback
- Score, streak, guess count, and level progression
- Fast minimalist puzzle rounds

EchoGrid is in early testing. Feedback is welcome as the sound field, difficulty curve, and level mechanics evolve.

## Early Beta Positioning

EchoGrid is an early beta puzzle game. The core hidden-target game is playable, while the sound field, difficulty curve, level mechanics, and visual polish are expected to evolve through tester feedback.
